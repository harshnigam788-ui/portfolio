(04) Notes from the work

Three stories worth telling.

Fig. 02 · The production machine (2026)

300+ pages a month, and none of them late.

Writers, designers, developers, QC and link builders, all on one tracker. Briefs go in, pages go live, QC checks every one after it ships, and each client can see exactly where their work stands. Boring on purpose.

Fig. 03 · AI visibility reporting (2026)

Rankings don't tell you what ChatGPT says about you. So I asked it.

I built reporting that runs real buyer questions through ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Copilot, then records whether the brand gets named, how it's described, and which sources get cited. Clients now get it every month, next to their Search Console numbers.

Fig. 04 · New business (2025-26)

60+ audits. Every one starts with a live crawl.

Before I pitch anything, I crawl the prospect's site and check how AI engines see them. The findings become tiered plans for HVAC, insurance, BFSI, real estate, healthcare, legal, manufacturing, logistics, renewables, retail, edtech, FMCG, SaaS and staffing.

(06) About

The short version.

I got into marketing in 2020 as a remote intern for Digital Ipsum in Singapore. My first campaigns grew the numbers 12% in a month, and I've been chasing that feeling ever since.

Then came three years at StockGro, running SEO, SEM and social, and writing about markets for people who'd rather not read about markets. Somewhere in there I finished a management degree at Jain University and got a paper on Zomato's IPO published.

I joined IInfotanks in 2024 to write content. By June 2026 I was running the team that ships it: writers, designers, developers, QC and link builders. Twenty-one people, ten-plus brands, two countries.

I still think content should be strategic, engaging and, dare I say, fun. It also has to rank. And these days, get cited.
